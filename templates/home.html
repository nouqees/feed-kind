Reduce Food Waste. Feed More People.

A simple platform connecting food donors with NGOs so that excess food can be collected and put to good use instead of being wasted.

1. Donors post food

Restaurants, hotels, halls and individuals list excess food that's ready for pickup.

2. NGOs request it

Registered NGOs browse available donations and request the ones they need.

3. Donor confirms handover

The donor accepts a request, the NGO collects the food, and the donor marks it complete.

Note: This platform does not certify food as safe. Donors and NGOs must independently assess whether food is suitable for donation and consumption.
